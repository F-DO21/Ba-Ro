Privacy Policy

Our Commitment:
We Value Your Privacy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

At Ba-ro, we value your privacy. We understand the importance of protecting your personal information, and we are committed to processing your data fairly, lawfully, and transparently in compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how it is used, and how we protect it when you browse our store or place an order.

Ba-ro operates as an online clothing store. To keep transactions as straightforward as possible, our site serves as an ordering catalog where customer orders are submitted directly via an email order form. We do not process direct payments or store financial payment gateway details on this platform.

What Data We Collect

When you interact with our store or place an order for our shirts, we may collect the following information:

  • Identity & Contact Data: Registered details like first name, last name, email address, contact number, and delivery address.
  • Order Detials Information regarding the specific shirt, sizes, and quantities selected via the order form.
  • Technical Data: Basic browser type, operating system, and passive session cookies, ONLY the strictly necessary, for website functionality and navigation.

How We Use Your Information

We collect and use your personal information strictly for the following operational purposes:

  1. To process and fulfill your orders submitted via email/order form.
  2. To contact you in regards to your order status, delivery, or inquiries.
  3. To maintain, support, and improve the user experience and layout of our online store.

Data Sharing

No data are sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing, advertising, or commercial purposes. Your personal information is kept strictly confidential within our team and is utilized solely for customer service and order fulfillment.

Data Security and Your Rights

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security safeguards to keep your personal data safe from unauthorized access or disclosure. As a user, you hold full rights under the Data Privacy Act, including:

  • The right to request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • The right to request correction or updating of inaccurate details.
  • The right to request the deletion or removal of your personal information from our records.

Contact Us About Privacy

For privacy-related questions, contact hello@bato.com.ph.
